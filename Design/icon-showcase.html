A molten-gold keystone locks the apex of a recessed bronze arch — the wedge that holds the whole structure together, a direct metaphor for the app that holds Vietnamese typing together. Carved into the stone's face is the dấu mũ (circumflex ^), engraved as a real bevelled groove with a warm bottom light, so the tone mark reads as the soul of the piece rather than an added sticker; its inverted-V also echoes the arch's apex. A single calligraphic brushstroke forms the dấu ngã (tilde) — the most expressive of Vietnamese tone marks and the "soul" of typing tiếng Việt — laid on a refined espresso-dark ground so the ink glows like gilded lacquer. The stroke tapers to real brush points with a wet top sheen for thư pháp heritage, a small gold tone-dot (dấu nặng) balances the composition, and a vermilion seal (triện/chop) in the corner carries a negative-space keystone wedge, tying the name and the idea of the piece that holds everything together. The warm ivory→gold ink on deep espresso with one vermilion accent is a cultured, ownable palette that deliberately avoids generic keyboard-app blue.

A single tactile keycap carries the letter "Ế" — the E with circumflex and acute tone — so the app instantly reads as a Vietnamese input method, with the diacritic stack as the true hero. The stacked marks are lifted into warm amber/gold, popping off an ivory keycap and its cool indigo→violet canvas, so the eye lands on the dấu first, then the key. The concave top light, soft lip shadow and embossed legend make the key feel physically pressable, while the bold forms keep it legible from 1024px down to a 32px Dock tile and the menu bar.

Bước tiếp theo (khi bạn đã chọn)

  1. Chốt 1 concept — file gốc nằm ở Design/icons/<key>.svg và <key>-menubar.svg.
  2. Xuất AppIcon: render SVG ra PNG 1024→16 và dựng AppIcon.appiconset (hoặc dùng Icon Composer của macOS 26/27).
  3. Glyph menu-bar: dùng bản đơn sắc làm template image (tự đổi màu theo sáng/tối), 2 trạng thái VN/EN.
  4. Mình có thể tinh chỉnh thêm concept bạn chọn (màu, độ dày nét, bo góc) cho hoàn hảo.
